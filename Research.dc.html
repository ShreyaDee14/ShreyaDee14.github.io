Graduate Researcher, Johns Hopkins University
Civil & Systems Engineering
Advisor: Prof. Michael Shields

Oct 2025 — Dec 2025

Renewable capacity across the EU-27

Graduate Researcher, Johns Hopkins University
Advisors: Daniel Kammen (JHU); Mariusz Baranowski, Piotr Jabkowski (Adam Mickiewicz University)

  • ✳Modeled the distribution of renewable energy capacities among EU-27 member states against the 2030 and 2050 climate targets under uncertainty.
  • ✳Extended a deterministic allocation framework into a stochastic simulation pipeline capturing policy, technological, weather and economic variability.
  • ✳Monte Carlo simulations generating probabilistic deployment scenarios within country-level feasibility constraints.
  • ✳Produced distributions of capacity outcomes and confidence intervals for national deployment targets.
Jan 2023 — Jun 2023

Portfolio optimisation of mutual funds

Undergraduate Researcher, Amity University Kolkata
Department of Statistics
Advisor: Soumya Banerjee (St. Xavier's College, Kolkata)

  • ✳Optimal investment strategies for debt and hybrid mutual funds from historical NAV data of top-performing AMFI-registered funds, 2018–2022.
  • ✳Quantitative risk assessment estimating Beta via CAPM regression — each fund's systematic exposure relative to the market index.
  • ✳Compared Markowitz mean–variance, Generalized Reduced Gradient, Particle Swarm Optimization and Genetic Algorithms for efficient-frontier allocations.
  • ✳ANN and LSTM forecasting models with train–validation–test splits, evaluated out-of-sample on RMSE and MAE.
